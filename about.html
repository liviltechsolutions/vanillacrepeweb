OUR STORY

A sweet idea,
layer by layer.

Vanilla Crepe began in 2014, founded by Nelson Liew with a love for mille crepe cakes. The brand brings an original French-inspired recipe home to Malaysia, where each cake is built from thin crepes and smooth cream.

Over the years, Vanilla Crepe has grown to 23 outlets nationwide and was recognised by the Malaysia Book of Records as Malaysia’s largest mille crepe chain. Its menu now spans classic and premium flavours for birthdays, reunions and everyday celebrations.

The goal remains simple: make Vanilla Crepe the cake people think of when they want to share a good moment.
